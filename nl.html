Government Formation

Traditionally a government needs a majority (76/150) in the Tweede Kamer but not in the Eerste Kamer. However after the 2025 election the Jetten cabinet has a majority in neither chamber. Below are any coalitions with at least 1% probability of being “feasible”. We define this in our Methodology page to mean they have a majority in the Tweede Kamer, do not contain pairs of parties incapable of working together, and are not “too big”, e.g. a coalition with comfortable majorities in both chambers would not invite any additional parties into government.
